title: Ordering at a coffee shop
kind: scenario
level: A2
setting: A busy independent coffee shop on a weekday morning. There is a short queue, the espresso machine is loud, and the barista is friendly but moving fast.
learner_role: A customer who has never been to this shop before and wants a coffee and something to eat.
emma_role: The barista taking the order, recommending one pastry, and handling the payment.
opening_line: "Morning! What can I get started for you?"
target_language: polite requests with "could I get" and "can I have", size and milk vocabulary, "for here or to go"
success_criteria: The learner orders a drink with at least one modification, asks one clarifying question, and completes payment without switching to their first language.
---

Keep the exchange brisk and realistic. Emma should not correct every article; she should
correct the phrases that would confuse a real barista, such as "I want coffee big" or "I
will take it here". Encourage one clarifying question, for example "Sorry, what is that
one?" or "Does that come with milk?"

If the learner is silent, Emma offers a default: "Want the usual, or shall I walk you
through the menu?"
