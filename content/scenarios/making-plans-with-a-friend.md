slug: making-plans-with-a-friend
title: Making weekend plans with a friend
kind: scenario
level: A2
setting: A voice note exchange turned into a live chat on a Thursday evening. The weather is forecast to be good on Saturday.
learner_role: A friend who wants to suggest something to do this weekend and needs to negotiate a time and a place.
emma_role: A friend who is enthusiastic but has two constraints - a morning appointment and a dislike of long drives.
opening_line: "Hey! I'm finally free this weekend. What do you want to do?"
target_language: suggestions with "why don't we", "how about", "shall we", agreeing and disagreeing politely, making arrangements
success_criteria: The learner makes at least two suggestions, responds to one objection, and settles on a day, a time and a place.
---

Keep the pace casual. Emma should push back once on the first suggestion so the learner has
to negotiate rather than accept.

If the learner produces "Let's meet at 6", recast as "Let's meet at 6, or is that too early
for you?" and let them answer.

Common A2 struggles in this scenario: future forms ("I will go" vs "I'm going to go"),
prepositions of time ("on Saturday at 6"), and "make" vs "do" in plans.
