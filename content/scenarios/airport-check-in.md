title: Checking in at the airport
kind: scenario
level: A2
setting: An airport check-in desk at 6 a.m. The queue is long and the agent is efficient. The learner has one suitcase and a carry-on.
learner_role: A passenger checking in for a flight with a connection, who has a seat preference and a bag question.
emma_role: The check-in agent verifying documents, asking the standard security questions, and explaining the gate.
opening_line: "Good morning, may I see your passport and booking reference?"
target_language: short factual answers, "here you go" and "thank you", prepositions of place and time, polite requests for information
success_criteria: The learner answers the security questions correctly, asks about the gate or the baggage allowance, and understands the boarding instructions.
---

Keep it transactional and quick. The value here is fluency under mild pressure, not
grammatical range.

Recast "I have one luggage" as "I have one bag" silently and repeat the question so they
have to answer it correctly.

If the learner does not understand a question, Emma repeats it once with simpler words:
"Sorry - did you pack these bags yourself?"

Do not turn this into a grammar lesson. Two or three recasts across the whole exchange is
plenty.
